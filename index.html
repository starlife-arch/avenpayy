Customer-to-business payments

Collect payments with STK Push.

C2B means customer-to-business: the customer pays your business. Your application initiates an M-PESA STK Push, the customer approves the prompt on their phone using their M-PESA PIN, and successful funds settle to your configured M-PESA till, bank, or Paybill.

Your server initiates the STK Push with the customer’s phone number and payment amount.
The customer reviews and approves the payment prompt on their phone.
Successful payment proceeds to your configured till, bank, or Paybill.
Confirm the final transaction result using a callback or status-check endpoint.
Read C2B / STK Push docs
STK Push payment flow REQUEST → APPROVE → SETTLE
01

Initiate STK Push

Your application sends a payment request for the customer and amount.

02

Customer approves

The customer enters their M-PESA PIN to authorize the payment.

03

Funds settle

Successful payment settles to your configured till, bank, or Paybill.

Do not treat the initial STK Push response as proof of payment. Confirm success with the callback or transaction-status endpoint before fulfilling an order.
Business-to-customer payments

Send money out with B2C payouts.

B2C means business-to-customer: your business sends money to a recipient. For example, you might send a refund, reward, commission, or another supported business payment to a customer’s mobile money account.

Your backend initiates a payout using the required recipient and payment details.
Check the payout status and use callbacks to process its final result.
Check your configured account and available balance before initiating payouts.
Read B2C payout docs
B2C payout flow INITIATE → TRACK → CONFIRM
01

Submit payout

Your backend sends the recipient, amount, and required request details.

02

Track progress

Save the transaction reference and check the payout status.

03

Handle result

Use the final response or callback to update your records.
